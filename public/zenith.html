Zenith brings an assessment center and a development center together.

Leadership decisions need evidence, and leadership growth takes months. Zenith connects both: the evidence that shows who is ready and where the gaps are, and the development that closes them.

The Edstellar 5A framework, from first assessment to a lasting pipeline.

Five phases, each with its own output. Edstellar consultants run every phase and set the timeline with you; the durations below are typical.

01

Assess1 to 2 weeks

Diagnose current leadership capability from several sources of evidence.

  • Edstellar consultants select the instruments that fit your use case
  • Leaders complete online psychometric and partner assessments
  • Managers, peers and direct reports give 360° feedback through Orbit
  • Certified Edstellar assessors run live exercises, workshops and team activities

Phase outputEvidence from every input, for every leader in scope.

02

Analyze2 to 3 weeks

Consolidate the evidence into one leadership view for each leader and the cohort.

  • Individual leadership profiles that bring every source together
  • Capability gap maps for each leader and for the whole cohort
  • Succession readiness insights for critical roles

Phase outputLeadership profiles, gap maps and succession readiness insights.

03

Architect3 to 5 weeks

Design individual and cohort development pathways from the evidence.

  • An individual development plan built from each leader's profile
  • Cohort pathways for the gaps leaders share
  • The right mix of curriculum, coaching, mentoring and job shadowing for each pathway

Phase outputIndividual development plans and cohort pathways.

04

Activate6 to 12 weeks

Deliver development that builds leadership capability in the role.

  • A university-certified leadership curriculum designed by Edstellar
  • Coaching and mentoring on each leader's development priorities
  • Job shadowing in the roles leaders are preparing for

Phase outputDevelopment delivered across every pathway.

05

AdvanceOngoing

Track progress, re-assess and sustain the leadership pipeline.

  • Progress tracked against each development plan
  • Re-assessment to show how capability has moved
  • The succession pipeline reviewed and refreshed over time

Phase outputProgress reports and a current view of the succession pipeline.

Zenith combines every source of evidence into one leadership view.

Edstellar consultants choose the instruments that fit each organization's use case, so every leader is seen through several lenses rather than a single test.

One leadership view.

Every input consolidated for each leader, then rolled up for the cohort.

  • Strengths confirmed across sourcesWhere tests, raters, assessors and performance data agree.
  • Gaps against the target roleCapability and leadership knowledge to build next.
  • Readiness for the next roleThe basis for succession, promotion and high-potential decisions.

  • Psychometric and partner assessments

    Online instruments selected by Edstellar consultants for your use case, from a vendor-neutral range.

  • Situational judgment tests

    Realistic leadership scenarios that show how each leader weighs options and makes calls typical of the target role.

  • 360° feedback through Orbit

    Structured feedback from managers, peers and direct reports, run on Orbit, Edstellar's 360-degree feedback tool.

  • Live assessment center activities

    Exercises, workshops and team activities, observed by certified Edstellar assessors.

  • Business simulations

    Case studies, in-basket exercises and business simulations that test strategic thinking and decision making under realistic pressure.

  • Performance and talent data

    Appraisal ratings, KPIs and talent review outcomes from your HR systems, so assessment evidence sits alongside track record.

Zenith leadership pathways that build over months.

Assessment is where a Zenith pathway starts. Each leader then moves through development designed from their own profile, step by step toward the next role.

Trail log

  1. 01

    Diagnostics

    Assessment and development center evidence sets each leader's starting point.

  2. 02

    University-certified curriculum

    A leadership curriculum designed by Edstellar through its university tie-ups.

  3. 03

    Coaching

    One-to-one Edstellar coaching on each leader's development priorities.

  4. 04

    Mentoring

    Guidance from experienced leaders on real decisions and the moves ahead.

  5. 05

    Job shadowing

    Leaders observe and take part in the work of the role they are preparing for.

The next role

Each pathway is set in the Architect phase and delivered in Activate, so the mix and pace follow the leader's profile.
