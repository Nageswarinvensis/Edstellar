Do’s and don’ts

What should I do?

Right and wrong side by side. Example: workplace safety.

Do

  • Follow the posted safety rules
  • Wear the right protective gear
  • Report hazards right away

Don’t

  • Use phones in restricted areas
  • Take shortcuts on procedures
  • Work alone in high-risk areas
