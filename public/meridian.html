      What is Meridian?

      Meridian is Edstellar’s AI-assisted platform for one-on-one leadership coaching. Each leader works with one certified coach through a structured track of six phases, in a private session room, while the organization runs the program from its own portal.

      Who is Meridian for?

      Organizations that want to coach a group of their leaders at the same time, from first-time managers to senior executives. The organization nominates each leader and names the objective; the coaching itself belongs to the leader.

      How are coaches chosen?

      Meridian ranks every available coach against the leader’s profile using seven weighted factors, after three hard rules. The leader and the program admin then choose together in a 30-minute session, and the leader and coach test the fit in a free chemistry session before any coaching begins.

      What does AI-assisted mean in Meridian?

      Meridian ranks coaches for each leader, suggests questions from the live discussion for the coach to use or set aside, and prepares a draft report after each session. The coach decides what is asked and approves every report before the leader sees it.

      Can the organization see what is discussed?

      No. The organization sees attendance, session dates, coach assignment, credit use and outcomes, never what is said, written or recorded in a session. At the end, the leader chooses what goes into the summary the organization receives.

      How long does a Meridian program run?

      A typical program is ten to twelve one-hour sessions, about every two weeks, completed over six to eight months. The organization sets the number of sessions for each leader.

      What happens if the coach is not the right fit?

      If chemistry shows the fit is wrong, the leader goes back to matching and keeps every session. Later in the program, the leader can ask Edstellar in confidence for a different coach; the organization is told only that a change is under way.

      Is Meridian delivered online?

      Yes. Every session runs in the Meridian video room in the browser, with reminders before each session and messages for practical things in between.
