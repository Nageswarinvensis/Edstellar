Orbit, answered.

What is Orbit?

Orbit is Edstellar's AI-empowered 360-degree feedback tool. It collects anonymous feedback from up to six rater groups, drafts each report with AI, and has an Edstellar consultant review every report before the person receiving feedback sees it.

Who reviews the AI-drafted reports?

An Edstellar consultant. They check each draft against the raw scores, remove comments that could identify a rater, refine the summary and development plan, add their own notes, and then approve and release the report. A released report can also be revoked.

How does Orbit protect rater anonymity?

Ratings appear only as group averages, never by name. Each rater group has a minimum number of raters (three is recommended), and groups below the minimum are combined with others or suppressed. Written comments reach the report as themes and quotes with no names or roles attached. Because a line manager is usually a group of one, the manager's rating appears as its own line in the report.

Who can give feedback in an Orbit 360?

Up to six rater groups: self, line manager, peers, direct reports, skip-level manager and external stakeholders such as clients, vendors and partners. Each assessee can have up to 10 participants, self included.

Can Orbit run 180-degree or 270-degree feedback?

Yes. Orbit runs 45, 90, 180, 270 and 360-degree feedback. Choose the rater groups for each engagement: a self-assessment for a 45, the line manager alone for a 90, self and line manager for a 180, self, line manager and peers for a 270, or all six groups for a 360.

Can we use our own competencies and questions?

Yes. Choose from the competency pool, each with behavioral items, add your own competencies and items, include skills rated on a proficiency scale, pick a rating scale from 3 to 10 points or set custom labels, and add your own open-ended questions for all raters or for one group. Orbit offers 16 ready use cases, and you can add your own.

What does the assessee receive?

Once the report is released, the assessee sees their strongest area, blind spot and hidden strength, their scores next to how others rated them, themes from written feedback, a development plan linked to Edstellar programs and a note from their Edstellar consultant, with a PDF to download.
